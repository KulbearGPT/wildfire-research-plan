Status distinctions

  • Measured negative/mixed: a valid evaluated comparison fails its primary, attribution, clean-input, transfer or compression criterion. Positive cells remain in the positive collection.
  • Invalid/confounded historical foundation: faulty pooled-year exposure or inherited initialization prevents a corrected-data efficacy claim; the raw observation is preserved.
  • Cancelled/unrun: no method efficacy conclusion is allowed. Includes Missingness-conditioned residual experts (X7) overlap cancellation, First-layer reliability calibration (X21)/Frozen-clean FireDrop distillation (X24) idea rejection, Temporal reliability fusion (D3) identifiability rejection, cancelled repairs and never-released confirmations.
  • Preparation/runtime failure or pending/unreported: failed data alignment, OOM, dependency failure and missing final results are not negative model results.

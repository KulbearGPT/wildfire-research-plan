Why are we changing protocols?

Official Fold 2 teaches reproduction of a published setup. The next project asks a different question: how training choices affect predictions under controlled missing observations. Its corrected preprocessing, longer training-year coverage and shared foundation define a separate comparison.

Completing Fold 2 is useful execution practice. Its checkpoint and score do not become row A of the project comparison. Browser-study students may continue with the worked example without claiming either practical is complete.

Keep two named records: official reproduction and project T1 continuation.

Obtain just the foundation you need

Provided foundation

Obtain B3, its checksum and completion/provenance record from the instructor. Verify the file identity, input contract and training/statistics identities. Copying a pathname is not verification.

Train your foundation

Use the T1-only foundation path. Train B3, export the selected checkpoint, then evaluate it. T5 missingness-trained foundation (B5) is a separate T5 extension and is not needed here.

Foundation B3 uses its best-validation selection rule. Later ERM/cosine branches select their final continuation step. Keep the two selection stages separate.

Reference · 4 / 4

Hand over a concrete asset sheet

  1. Record project source/environment and site-configuration locations.
  2. Record corrected data identity, split and training-only statistics identity.
  3. Record B3 path, checksum, completion record and selection rule.
  4. Declare T1, seed 0, 3,000 updates per continuation, batch 64 and final-step selection. Preserve shared objective, optimizer settings and augmentation; only cosine changes the schedule.
  5. Declare the same 2021 samples and four missingness conditions for A/B/C, and distinct output locations for each branch.

Proceed to ERM execution only when the real asset fields are filled and verified. If a field is missing, continue the teaching exercise with status “not executed”, and use the stuck guide.

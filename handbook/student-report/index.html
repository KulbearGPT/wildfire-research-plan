Read the right files and fields

For project continuation, keep checkpoint.pt, summary.json, job logs and source/configuration records together. The summary describes evaluation; it does not alone prove training completed or that the initializations matched.

Check year=2021. Continuation summaries also identify history, method and seed. Match these to the experiment record; use provenance and checkpoint metadata to verify training and initialization separately.

Why do the fields differ?

The foundation evaluator nests each scenario's metrics; the continuation runner places the metrics directly inside the scenario. These are different output schemas. Their delta_avg_precision_from_m00, when present, compares a scenario with clean inputs within one model; it is not B−A or C−B.

Verified against foundation evaluator and continuation runner. Official Fold 2 follows its own output guide and is not a row in this table.

For every cell, record source filename, scenario and field. Leave absent values missing; never replace them with zero.

Read an acceptable teaching report

Question and prediction

Does cosine scheduling improve Primary AP over matched constant-rate ERM? C−B ≤ 0 would weaken the prediction for this run.

Declared comparison

The teaching plan uses T1, seed 0, the same B3, data/statistics and 2021 population. B and C start independently with fresh optimizers, batch 64 and 3,000 updates; final checkpoints are selected. The schedule is the intended difference.

Evidence and execution status

Not executed. Inputs are the explicitly synthetic excerpts linked above. No real checkpoint, job ID, completion log or checksum exists for this example. A practical report must replace this section with actual artifact identities and completion evidence.

Result and interpretation

In the supplied teaching table, cosine exceeds ERM by .03 Primary AP and falls by .01 clean AP. Its .09 improvement over the foundation includes .06 from the ordinary continuation comparison. This supports the calculation and interpretation exercise, not an empirical claim about the method.

Limit and next comparison

A real single-seed result would still require matched repetitions before claiming stability. Plan additional matched seeds with a fixed metric and resource budget; do not select favorable test results after seeing them.

Reference · 4 / 4

Check your report before handing it over

Find the flaws: “Cosine gains .09, therefore the schedule is better everywhere.”

.09 compares C to the frozen foundation, not to ERM. The schedule comparison is .03. Clean AP falls by .01 relative to ERM, so “everywhere” is false. With invented values, neither number establishes a real effect. If the candidate instead started from ERM's final checkpoint, the extra training would also invalidate the intended attribution.

  1. Identity: can a reader locate every real input and output, or see that this is a teaching-only exercise?
  2. Comparison: are the intended difference and matched settings explicit?
  3. Completeness: are all four conditions, missing measurements and regressions shown?
  4. Reasoning: is C−B separated from C−A, with limitations?
  5. Execution: does a claimed practical completion include actual updates, checkpoint and evaluation evidence?

A valid no-improvement result passes. Missing provenance or unmatched branches means the practical comparison is incomplete, even when the number is high. Return to lesson 7 submission.

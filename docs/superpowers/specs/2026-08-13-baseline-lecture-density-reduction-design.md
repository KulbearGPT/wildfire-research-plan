# Baseline Lecture Density Reduction Design

## Goal

Make the English-only baseline reproduction lecture easier to scan without changing its scientific content, visual identity, navigation, or verified numerical claims.

## Approved direction

Use a structural reduction rather than a cosmetic rewrite:

- reduce prose by approximately 15–20%;
- reduce card-like content blocks from about 27 uses to 16–18;
- keep the four hero metrics, beginner vocabulary, the twelve-fold results, scientific evidence boundaries, and the final reusable checklist;
- turn repeated conceptual card grids into short numbered lists, compact tables, or continuous prose;
- keep at most one primary visual component per section wherever practical;
- preserve the exact section order, English-only presentation, color system, responsive behavior, theme toggle, search, print support, and navigation.

## Content hierarchy

Cards remain only when spatial separation communicates a real comparison, definition, metric, or claim boundary. Explanatory steps that form one continuous argument should read as one continuous argument. Exact experimental values and provenance statements remain unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The rendered lecture remains visually consistent with Lecture 02.
2. The page contains all 16 existing section IDs and the same verified fold metrics and aggregates.
3. Approximate visible English word count falls by 15–20% from the original baseline of about 2,819 words.
4. Card-like blocks fall from about 27 to between 16 and 18.
5. Desktop and mobile layouts have no page-level horizontal overflow.
6. Search, sidebar navigation, dark mode, and print CSS continue to work.

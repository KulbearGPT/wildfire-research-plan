Try it yourself

Which result is a training reproduction?

Reason through the question and every option

How to reason: Ask which part of the pipeline was actually reproduced. Evaluating existing weights exercises loading, preprocessing and scoring. Reproducing training additionally exercises initialization, optimization and checkpoint selection.

  • Testing a downloaded checkpoint — not the answer. A downloaded checkpoint already contains someone else’s training outcome. Evaluating it is a useful released-weight evaluation, but it does not show that your training procedure can produce a checkpoint.
  • Training your own checkpoint under the frozen official contract — correct. Training under the frozen official contract exercises the process the task asks you to reproduce. Keep initialization, data split, update budget and selection rules fixed, and retain completion and evaluation artifacts.

Take away: Name the evidence precisely: released-weight evaluation, smoke check or completed training reproduction. Running training alone does not guarantee matching the published score.

Try it yourself

Slurm returned a job ID and the job is PENDING. What should you do?

Reason through the question and every option

How to reason: Follow the job lifecycle: submitted, queued, running, then a terminal state. A job ID identifies a scheduler record. PENDING says the job is waiting; it supplies no evidence of completed model updates.

  • Record it and monitor the existing job — correct. Record the existing job ID and inspect its queue reason and status. If it cannot start because of a configuration issue, diagnose that issue before deciding whether a replacement is needed.
  • Submit another identical job — not the answer. Another identical submission does not repair the first job. It can leave two jobs queued and later duplicate work or compete for the same outputs.
  • Report successful training — not the answer. The scheduler accepted the submission, but training may not have started. A success claim requires a terminal state, actual completed updates and the expected checkpoint and evaluation artifacts.

Take away: Monitor one identified job. Distinguish accepted submission, successful process exit and a verified experiment result.

Check all three: terminal state/exit code, actual completed updates, and the selected checkpoint plus evaluation artifacts. A successful process exit alone cannot replace artifact checks.

Useful scheduler checks
squeue -j JOB_ID
sacct -j JOB_ID --format=JobID,State,ExitCode,Elapsed,MaxRSS

Replace JOB_ID with your recorded ID. Preserve the first failing log and classify the failure before resubmitting.

Done when: the run completed its contract and its outputs can be located.

One trained fold

One split and your learned weights. It is not a twelve-fold mean.

Released-weight evaluation

Checks whether published weights execute here. It does not mean you retrained them.

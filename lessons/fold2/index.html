Step 2 · Prepare

Make your own environment and script bundle.

First complete the access and preparation gate. No cluster access yet? Follow the browser study route and label your Fold 2 record as a plan.

Use your own account, accessible storage and the site’s actual modules/GPU names. Keep a notebook of paths and job IDs.

  1. Configure your directories and accounts.
  2. Create all self-contained scripts in section 2. These files are dependencies for later jobs.
  3. Submit environment and official-code setup.

Bring back: config, pinned source revision, compatibility patch and environment freeze. The independent tutorial does not require this project’s model code or a teacher’s checkpoint.

Installation and downloads belong in the allowed Slurm setup path. A new cluster may need different accounts, modules or network access; historical hardware measurements are not resource guarantees.

Done when: setup has completed successfully and you can identify the exact code/environment used.

Step 4 · Train

Submission is not completion.

Follow the full Fold 2 training step. Keep the declared budget; do not stop or extend it to chase the reference number.

Try it yourself

Slurm returned a job ID and the job is PENDING. What should you do?

Check your reasoning

Record and monitor the existing job. PENDING is a queue state, not a failed run and not completed training.

Check all three: terminal state/exit code, actual completed updates, and the selected checkpoint plus evaluation artifacts. A successful process exit alone cannot replace artifact checks.

Useful scheduler checks
squeue -j JOB_ID
sacct -j JOB_ID --format=JobID,State,ExitCode,Elapsed,MaxRSS

Replace JOB_ID with your recorded ID. Preserve the first failing log and classify the failure before resubmitting.

Done when: the run completed its contract and its outputs can be located.

Step 5 · Interpret

Compare the right result with the right reference.

Keep these three quantities separate: your trained Fold 2 score, the released Fold 2 checkpoint score, and the paper’s multi-fold summary.

One trained fold

One split and your learned weights. It is not a twelve-fold mean.

Released-weight evaluation

Checks whether published weights execute here. It does not mean you retrained them.

The twelve released checkpoints and full twelve-fold training are optional extensions after your first fold, not required classroom work.

Done when: you can state what your number measures and why different protocols are not method gains.

Step 6 · Submit

Hand over a run another student can audit.

  1. Record the source commit, environment, data preparation and official split.
  2. Record seed, batch, precision, budget and checkpoint selection.
  3. Attach job IDs, final states, logs, checkpoint and metric paths.
  4. Explain differences from the reference without tuning on the test score.

Negative, mixed, invalid and unrun experiment archive

This archive records what failed and what was never tested. A failed strict gate does not imply absence of a positive signal: see positive-signals.md. Retained mechanism details, controls and original numeric outcomes remain in the linked ledgers and source commits. Reproduction/qualification status is separate from scientific result status.

Status distinctions

  • Measured negative/mixed: a valid evaluated comparison fails its primary, attribution, clean-input, transfer or compression criterion. Positive cells remain in the positive collection.
  • Invalid/confounded historical foundation: faulty pooled-year exposure or inherited initialization prevents a corrected-data efficacy claim; the raw observation is preserved.
  • Cancelled/unrun: no method efficacy conclusion is allowed. Includes X7 overlap cancellation, X21/X24 idea rejection, D3 identifiability rejection, cancelled repairs and never-released confirmations.
  • Preparation/runtime failure or pending/unreported: failed data alignment, OOM, dependency failure and missing final results are not negative model results.

Words you will meet in the practical

Checkpoint: saved model state. Seed: a recorded random-number initialization. Optimizer update: one parameter update, not one epoch. Smoke run: a short execution check, not a performance result. Artifact: a saved output such as a log, metric file or checkpoint. Normalization statistics: preprocessing values computed from training data.
